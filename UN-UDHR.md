**Article 2:**

without distinction of any kind, 
such as race, colour, sex, language, religion, political or other opinion, 
national or social origin, property, birth or other status.
Furthermore, no distinction shall be made on the basis of 
the political, jurisdictional or international status of the country or territory to which a person belongs, 
whether it be independent, trust, non-self-governing 
or under any other limitation of sovereignty.
